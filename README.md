# Öwrenje — privacy policy

The privacy policy for the **Öwrenje** language-learning app, published at
<https://ysmayyl-coder.github.io/owrenje-privacy/> and linked from the app's
Google Play listing.

These pages are generated — do not edit them here. The source is
`store/privacy/*.md` in the app repository, and the site is rebuilt with:

    python3 store/tools/build_privacy_site.py --email <contact address>

A policy still marked DRAFT is deliberately left out of the build, so an
unreviewed translation can never appear on this site.
