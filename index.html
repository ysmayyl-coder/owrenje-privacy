Privacy Policy — Öwrenje

Last updated: 19 September 2026

The short version

The app has no accounts and asks for no permissions. Everything it records about your learning stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your progress: which lessons you have finished, your XP and your daily streak.
  • Usage events: that a lesson was started or completed, whether an answer was right or wrong, and which screens were opened. These are counts and timestamps about the app, not about you.
  • An anonymous install identifier: a random number generated on your device the first time the app runs. It is not your phone's serial number, not an advertising identifier, and it cannot be linked to you or followed into any other app. Reinstalling the app produces a new one.
  • Your answer to the one-time feedback question, if you choose to write one.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • It does not contain advertising, and no advertising or analytics company receives anything from it.
  • It does not use Google Play Services or any social login.

Sending data to us

The current version of the app sends nothing off your device.

A future version will send the usage events and anonymous install identifier described above to the developer's own server, over an encrypted connection, so that we can see how many people finish a lesson and come back the next day. That version will say so in its release notes, and this policy will be updated on the same day. Even then, no personal information is sent, nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold.

Children

The app is suitable for children and is meant to be used by them. It asks for no personal information from anyone, of any age: there is no account, no email address, no name, and no permission request of any kind.

Two details a parent may want to know:

  • The anonymous install identifier is a random number created on the device. It is used only to count how many people finish a lesson and come back. It cannot identify a child, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Once, after the first finished lesson, the app asks what could be better and offers a box to type in. Nothing has to be written there, and it can be closed with one tap. If a child does write something, it is kept on the phone and, in a future version, sent only to the developer.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app permanently removes everything described here from your phone. There is no account to close and nothing held anywhere else.
